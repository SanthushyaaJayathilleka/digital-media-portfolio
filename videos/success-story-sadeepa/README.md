# 🎬 Success Story of Sadeepa

## About the Project

This inspiring success story was developed under the Australian Aid-funded **Skills for Inclusive Growth (S4IG)** initiative, highlighting the journey of Sadeepa, a differently-abled youth from Sri Lanka.

Through the S4IG program, Sadeepa received specialized vocational training designed for differently-abled youth and progressed toward employment in the IT sector. His journey reflects the impact of inclusive training and employment opportunities.

## My Role

**Video Editing**

I contributed to the editing and production of this success-story video, helping shape the footage into a compelling narrative that highlights Sadeepa's journey and achievements.
