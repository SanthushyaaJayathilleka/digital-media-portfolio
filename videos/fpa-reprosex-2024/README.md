# 🎬 The FPA Sri Lanka – ReproSex 2024 

## About the Project

A video series documenting the **ReproSex 2024**, organized by FPA Sri Lanka.

The project involved editing and preparing video content for digital publication and managing the distribution of the videos across official social media platforms.

## My Role

### 🎥 Video Editing
Edited and prepared the ReproSex 2024 Opening Ceremony video series for digital publication.

### 📱 Digital Content Management
Managed the publishing of videos on the official **ReproSex 2024 YouTube channel, LinkedIn, Tiktok and Facebook page**, coordinating the prepared video content for online distribution.

## Project Details

- **Organization:** FPA Sri Lanka
- **Event:** ReproSex 2024
- **Role:** Video Editor & Digital Content Management
